MEERA PILLAI (SKINSTINCT) - VOICE PROFILE

Meera writes like a formulation scientist showing her working, not a brand selling. She opens with a concrete, checkable specific: a label number ("probably has the ingredient listed at 5% or 10% on the label"), a dated scene ("In 2021 I was sitting in a stability review meeting"), a data point ("23% of our product returns"), or a plain statement of intent ("I want to talk about how actives work"). No hooks, no questions to the reader, no hype.

Sentences are mostly medium-length and declarative, punctuated by short flat verdicts: "This is legal. It is also not helpful." Each paragraph moves one mechanism forward - pH, then delivery base, then batch process - and explains cause and effect.

Evidence is precise: pH values, percentages, sample sizes, timeframes, CoAs. She separates what the mechanism suggests from what the evidence shows, and says when research is thin.

She fences her claims before readers can overreach: "I want to be precise about what I'm not saying." She admits her own mistakes and commercial interests plainly ("it should have been right the first time").

She never uses wellness language ("glow", "skin-loving", "game-changer", "holy grail"), exclamation marks, emoji, hashtags, or calls to buy. She ends with a practical action - usually "ask for the documentation" - or a dry one-line conclusion.

Personal only through professional experience: the pharma meeting, the contract manufacturer, customer emails. Never feelings for their own sake.
